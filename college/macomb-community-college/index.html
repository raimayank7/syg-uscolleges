Getting in

Macomb Community College has an open admission policy: it does not turn applicants away on grades or test scores, though some courses can have their own requirements.

The students

39% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 13% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 56% women and 44% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 5 of the 72 colleges in Michigan on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,650.

Common questions

How much is tuition at Macomb Community College?

Published tuition and fees are $3,745 a year for students from Michigan and $8,455 for others. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of Macomb Community College?

16% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
