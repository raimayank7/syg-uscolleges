Western Connecticut State University: cost, admission and results

Western Connecticut State University is a public college in Danbury, Connecticut. It has about 3,511 undergraduate students. It admits 87% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $16,754 a year.

Getting in

Western Connecticut State University admits 87% of those who apply, which makes it open to most applicants. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%. The average SAT score of admitted students is 1110 out of 1600.

The students

42% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 44% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 50% women and 50% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 12 of the 29 colleges in Connecticut on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 2,693.

Common questions

What is the acceptance rate of Western Connecticut State University?

Western Connecticut State University admits 87% of applicants, according to the College Scorecard.

How much is tuition at Western Connecticut State University?

Published tuition and fees are $13,401 a year for students from Connecticut and $16,901 for others. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of Western Connecticut State University?

51% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
