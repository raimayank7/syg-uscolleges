Alice Lloyd College: cost, admission and results

Alice Lloyd College is a private nonprofit college in Pippa Passes, Kentucky. It has about 558 undergraduate students. It admits 75% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $18,367 a year.

Getting in

Alice Lloyd College admits 75% of those who apply, which makes it open to most applicants. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%. The average SAT score of admitted students is 1015 out of 1600 (midpoints: 505 in reading and writing, 445 in maths). The midpoint ACT score is 20 out of 36.

The students

63% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 27% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 51% women and 49% men.
