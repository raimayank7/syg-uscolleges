Professional University Dr. Carlos J. Borrero Rios: cost, admission and results

Professional University Dr. Carlos J. Borrero Rios is a private for-profit college in Hato Rey, Puerto Rico. It has about 7 undergraduate students. The average net price for students with federal aid is $10,615 a year.

The net price is what students who got federal aid paid on average for a year after grants and scholarships were taken off. It is 16% above the median net price of colleges in Puerto Rico on this site ($9,140). International students normally pay the published tuition and do not receive US federal aid.

The students

83% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 33% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 43% women and 57% men.
