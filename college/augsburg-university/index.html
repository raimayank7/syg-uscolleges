Augsburg University: cost, admission and results

Augsburg University is a private nonprofit college in Minneapolis, Minnesota. It has about 2,494 undergraduate students. It admits 82% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $23,390 a year.

Getting in

Augsburg University admits 82% of those who apply, which makes it open to most applicants. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%. The midpoint ACT score is 21 out of 36.

The students

62% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 77% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 55% women and 45% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 20 of the 61 colleges in Minnesota on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,878.

Common questions

What is the acceptance rate of Augsburg University?

Augsburg University admits 82% of applicants, according to the College Scorecard.

How much is tuition at Augsburg University?

Published tuition and fees are $46,840 a year. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of Augsburg University?
