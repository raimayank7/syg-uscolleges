Reading this list

  • A low admission rate shows how many people apply for each place. It is not a measure of teaching quality.
  • Some very selective colleges have low net prices, because they give large grants to students from lower-income families. Those grants are often not open to international students.
  • Colleges that do not report an admission rate, or have fewer than 500 undergraduates, are left out.

Source: College Scorecard, U.S. Department of Education, data released on 2 November 2026; checked in November 2026. Net price, debt and earnings describe students who received US federal aid. Costs change each year. This is official data for general information, not advice: confirm costs and admission rules on the college's own website and at collegescorecard.ed.gov.
