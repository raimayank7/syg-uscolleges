Montana Technological University: cost, admission and results

Montana Technological University is a public college in Butte, Montana. It has about 1,480 undergraduate students. It admits 91% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $17,205 a year.

Getting in

Montana Technological University admits 91% of those who apply, which makes it open to most applicants. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%. The average SAT score of admitted students is 1126 out of 1600 (midpoints: 600 in reading and writing, 590 in maths). The midpoint ACT score is 24 out of 36.

The students

29% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 38% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 37% women and 63% men.

Common questions

What is the acceptance rate of Montana Technological University?

Montana Technological University admits 91% of applicants, according to the College Scorecard.

How much is tuition at Montana Technological University?

Published tuition and fees are $8,770 a year for students from Montana and $26,570 for others. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of Montana Technological University?

61% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
