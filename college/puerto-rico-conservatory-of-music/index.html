Puerto Rico Conservatory of Music: cost, admission and results

Puerto Rico Conservatory of Music is a public college in San Juan, Puerto Rico. It has about 307 undergraduate students. It admits 78% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $8,025 a year.

The students

55% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 9% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 30% women and 70% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 43 of the 58 colleges in Puerto Rico on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 662.
