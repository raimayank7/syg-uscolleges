Miller-Motte College-Tulsa: cost, admission and results

Miller-Motte College-Tulsa is a private for-profit college in Tulsa, Oklahoma. It has about 389 undergraduate students. The average net price for students with federal aid is $22,285 a year.

Getting in

Miller-Motte College-Tulsa has an open admission policy: it does not turn applicants away on grades or test scores, though some courses can have their own requirements.

The students

80% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 69% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 92% women and 8% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 37 of the 42 colleges in Oklahoma on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,693.
