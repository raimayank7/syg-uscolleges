Suffolk University: cost, admission and results

Suffolk University is a private nonprofit college in Boston, Massachusetts. It has about 4,268 undergraduate students. It admits 82% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $40,008 a year.

Getting in

Suffolk University admits 82% of those who apply, which makes it open to most applicants. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%. The average SAT score of admitted students is 1216 out of 1600 (midpoints: 600 in reading and writing, 580 in maths). The midpoint ACT score is 26 out of 36.

The students

36% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 60% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 64% women and 36% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 22 of the 88 colleges in Massachusetts on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 2,300.

Common questions

What is the acceptance rate of Suffolk University?

Suffolk University admits 82% of applicants, according to the College Scorecard.

How much is tuition at Suffolk University?

Published tuition and fees are $49,556 a year. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of Suffolk University?

59% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
