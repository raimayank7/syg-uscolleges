Alaska Christian College: cost, admission and results

Alaska Christian College is a private nonprofit college in Soldotna, Alaska. It has about 44 undergraduate students. It admits 92% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $5,177 a year.

Getting in

Alaska Christian College admits 92% of those who apply, which makes it open to most applicants. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%.

The students

65% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 0% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 57% women and 43% men.

Common questions

What is the acceptance rate of Alaska Christian College?

Alaska Christian College admits 92% of applicants, according to the College Scorecard.
