Watts College of Nursing: cost, admission and results

Watts College of Nursing is a private nonprofit college in Durham, North Carolina. It has about 124 undergraduate students.

The students

26% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 64% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 81% women and 19% men.
