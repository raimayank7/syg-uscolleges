West Valley College: cost, admission and results

West Valley College is a public college in Saratoga, California. It has about 6,416 undergraduate students. The average net price for students with federal aid is $1,033 a year.

Getting in

West Valley College has an open admission policy: it does not turn applicants away on grades or test scores, though some courses can have their own requirements.

The net price is what students who got federal aid paid on average for a year after grants and scholarships were taken off (for a public college, students from inside the state). It is 95% below the median net price of colleges in California on this site ($22,336). International students are normally charged the out-of-state tuition and do not receive US federal aid.

The students

15% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 0% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 56% women and 44% men.

Common questions

How much is tuition at West Valley College?

Published tuition and fees are $0 a year for students from California and $11,244 for others. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of West Valley College?

52% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
