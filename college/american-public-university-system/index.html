American Public University System: cost, admission and results

American Public University System is a private for-profit college in Charles Town, West Virginia. It has about 40,074 undergraduate students. The average net price for students with federal aid is $11,116 a year.

Getting in

American Public University System has an open admission policy: it does not turn applicants away on grades or test scores, though some courses can have their own requirements.

What it costs

Tuition and fees for a year$8,400
Average full cost of a year (tuition, living, books), before aid$19,691
Average net price after grants and scholarships$11,116

The net price is what students who got federal aid paid on average for a year after grants and scholarships were taken off. It is about the same as the median net price of colleges in West Virginia on this site ($11,123). International students normally pay the published tuition and do not receive US federal aid.

The students

29% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 13% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 36% women and 64% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 1 of the 33 colleges in West Virginia on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,014.

Common questions

How much is tuition at American Public University System?

Published tuition and fees are $8,400 a year. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of American Public University System?

60% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
