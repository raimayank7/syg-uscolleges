The colleges of Colorado in brief

Of the 38 colleges listed for Colorado, 17 are public, 9 are private nonprofit and 12 are private for-profit. By level, 23 mainly award bachelor's degrees, 9 mainly award two-year associate degrees and 6 mainly award graduate degrees. Together they teach about 190,138 undergraduates, 74% of them at public colleges. The three largest, University of Colorado Boulder, Colorado Technical University-Colorado Springs and Colorado State University-Fort Collins, hold 45% of the total, and the middle-sized college has 3,079 undergraduates. The towns with the most colleges are Denver (11), Colorado Springs (6) and Aurora (3).

What college costs in Colorado

At the public colleges, the median published tuition and fees are $10,345 a year for students from Colorado and $22,564 for students from elsewhere. At the private nonprofit colleges the median is $45,299. Published prices are not what most families pay. Counting colleges with at least 300 undergraduates, the lowest average net price after grants is at Community College of Aurora ($3,714) and Pikes Peak State College ($4,709) and the highest at Colorado School of Mines ($32,901) and Rocky Mountain College of Art and Design ($32,562). The median net price across the state, $18,422, is 6% above the national median of $17,357.

Getting in

18 of the 38 colleges report an admission rate. The hardest to get into are United States Air Force Academy (14% admitted), Colorado College (18% admitted) and Colorado School of Mines (61% admitted). 15 of them admit at least seven applicants in ten. 12 colleges have an open admission policy and report no rate: they do not turn applicants away on grades or test scores.

Finishing and earning

Among colleges with at least 300 undergraduates, the highest share of students finishing their course is at Colorado College (86%), United States Air Force Academy (84%) and Colorado School of Mines (82%). The median graduation rate of the state's colleges is 47%, against 49% for the country. Ten years after starting, former students with federal aid earn the most at Colorado School of Mines ($102,077), Denver College of Nursing ($90,917) and Colorado State University Global ($82,047). The state median for that measure is $55,168, 4% above the national $53,132. Students who finish leave with a median federal loan debt of $20,500 (national median $19,994). Earnings follow the subjects a college teaches as much as its quality, so compare like with like.

Questions about colleges in Colorado

How many colleges are there in Colorado?

This site lists 38 open, degree-granting colleges and universities in Colorado, out of 3,161 on the whole site. Schools that mainly give short certificates are not counted.

What is the largest college in Colorado?

University of Colorado Boulder in Boulder, with about 31,939 undergraduate students.

Which college in Colorado has the lowest net price?

Among colleges with at least 300 undergraduates, Community College of Aurora: students with federal aid paid an average of $3,714 a year after grants. Students from other states or countries usually pay more.
