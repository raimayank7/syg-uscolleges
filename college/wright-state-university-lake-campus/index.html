Wright State University-Lake Campus: cost, admission and results

Wright State University-Lake Campus is a public college in Celina, Ohio. It has about 892 undergraduate students. The average net price for students with federal aid is $9,533 a year.

Getting in

Wright State University-Lake Campus has an open admission policy: it does not turn applicants away on grades or test scores, though some courses can have their own requirements. The midpoint ACT score is 21 out of 36.

The students

24% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 21% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 61% women and 39% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 63 of the 117 colleges in Ohio on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,006.

Common questions

How much is tuition at Wright State University-Lake Campus?

Published tuition and fees are $8,230 a year for students from Ohio and $18,414 for others. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of Wright State University-Lake Campus?

44% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
