Kent State University at Geauga: cost, admission and results

Kent State University at Geauga is a public college in Burton, Ohio. It has about 1,601 undergraduate students. The average net price for students with federal aid is $10,594 a year.

Getting in

Kent State University at Geauga has an open admission policy: it does not turn applicants away on grades or test scores, though some courses can have their own requirements.

The students

30% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 31% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 64% women and 36% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 42 of the 117 colleges in Ohio on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,006.

Common questions

How much is tuition at Kent State University at Geauga?

Published tuition and fees are $7,716 a year for students from Ohio and $17,960 for others. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of Kent State University at Geauga?

26% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
