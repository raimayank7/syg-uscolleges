The colleges of Alaska in brief

Of the 5 colleges listed for Alaska, 2 are public and 3 are private nonprofit. By level, 3 mainly award bachelor's degrees and 2 mainly award two-year associate degrees. Together they teach about 11,944 undergraduates, 96% of them at public colleges. The three largest, University of Alaska Anchorage, University of Alaska Fairbanks and Alaska Pacific University, hold 99% of the total, and the middle-sized college has 457 undergraduates. With so few colleges, each can be named: University of Alaska Anchorage in Anchorage is public, with about 7,212 undergraduates and published tuition and fees of $7,966 a year. University of Alaska Fairbanks in Fairbanks is public, with about 4,207 undergraduates and published tuition and fees of $8,956 a year. Alaska Pacific University in Anchorage is private nonprofit, with about 457 undergraduates and published tuition and fees of $20,760 a year. Alaska Christian College in Soldotna is private nonprofit, with about 44 undergraduates and published tuition and fees of $11,114 a year. Alaska Bible College in Palmer is private nonprofit, with about 24 undergraduates and published tuition and fees of $9,700 a year.

What college costs in Alaska

At the private nonprofit colleges the median is $11,114. The median net price across the state, $12,040, is 31% below the national median of $17,357.

Getting in

2 of the 5 colleges report an admission rate. 3 colleges have an open admission policy and report no rate: they do not turn applicants away on grades or test scores.

Finishing and earning

The median graduation rate of the state's colleges is 37%, against 49% for the country. The state median for that measure is $51,613, about the same as the national $53,132. Students who finish leave with a median federal loan debt of $19,375 (national median $19,994). Earnings follow the subjects a college teaches as much as its quality, so compare like with like.

Questions about colleges in Alaska

How many colleges are there in Alaska?

This site lists 5 open, degree-granting colleges and universities in Alaska, out of 3,161 on the whole site. Schools that mainly give short certificates are not counted.

What is the largest college in Alaska?

University of Alaska Anchorage in Anchorage, with about 7,212 undergraduate students.
