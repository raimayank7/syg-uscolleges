Dominican University New York: cost, admission and results

Dominican University New York is a private nonprofit college in Orangeburg, New York. It has about 1,105 undergraduate students. It admits 63% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $25,971 a year.

The students

49% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 72% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 63% women and 37% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 138 of the 254 colleges in New York on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,272.
