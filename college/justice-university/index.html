Justice University: cost, admission and results

Justice University is a private nonprofit college in Chandler, Arizona. It has about 116 undergraduate students. The average net price for students with federal aid is $9,430 a year.

Getting in

Justice University has an open admission policy: it does not turn applicants away on grades or test scores, though some courses can have their own requirements.

The students

43% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 10% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 34% women and 66% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 30 of the 33 colleges in Arizona on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,165.

What is not reported

The College Scorecard has no figure for an admission rate, a graduation rate, earnings of former students or loan debt at Justice University. That is common for colleges that are small or new, that teach mostly graduate students, or that take few first-time, full-time students, because several measures count only that group. It says nothing by itself about quality. Ask the college directly for the figures that matter to you.
