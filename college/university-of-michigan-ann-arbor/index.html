University of Michigan-Ann Arbor: cost, admission and results

University of Michigan-Ann Arbor is a public college in Ann Arbor, Michigan. It has about 34,177 undergraduate students. It admits 16% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $14,362 a year.

Getting in

University of Michigan-Ann Arbor admits 16% of those who apply, which makes it selective. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%. The average SAT score of admitted students is 1465 out of 1600.

The students

22% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 24% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 54% women and 46% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 2 of the 72 colleges in Michigan on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,650.

Common questions

What is the acceptance rate of University of Michigan-Ann Arbor?

University of Michigan-Ann Arbor admits 16% of applicants, according to the College Scorecard.

How much is tuition at University of Michigan-Ann Arbor?

Published tuition and fees are $18,346 a year for students from Michigan and $63,962 for others. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of University of Michigan-Ann Arbor?

93% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
