University of Virginia-Main Campus: cost, admission and results

University of Virginia-Main Campus is a public college in Charlottesville, Virginia. It has about 17,597 undergraduate students. It admits 17% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $18,056 a year.

Getting in

University of Virginia-Main Campus admits 17% of those who apply, which makes it selective. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%. The average SAT score of admitted students is 1480 out of 1600 (midpoints: 720 in reading and writing, 720 in maths). The midpoint ACT score is 34 out of 36.

The students

19% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 19% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 56% women and 44% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 7 of the 72 colleges in Virginia on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,217.

Common questions

What is the acceptance rate of University of Virginia-Main Campus?

University of Virginia-Main Campus admits 17% of applicants, according to the College Scorecard.

How much is tuition at University of Virginia-Main Campus?

Published tuition and fees are $22,431 a year for students from Virginia and $61,457 for others. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.
