Eastern Illinois University: cost, admission and results

Eastern Illinois University is a public college in Charleston, Illinois. It has about 4,188 undergraduate students. It admits 65% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $12,717 a year.

Getting in

Eastern Illinois University admits 65% of those who apply, which makes it moderately selective. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%. The average SAT score of admitted students is 1000 out of 1600 (midpoints: 520 in reading and writing, 500 in maths). The midpoint ACT score is 20 out of 36.

The students

31% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 30% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 60% women and 40% men.

Common questions

What is the acceptance rate of Eastern Illinois University?

Eastern Illinois University admits 65% of applicants, according to the College Scorecard.

How much is tuition at Eastern Illinois University?

Published tuition and fees are $14,654 a year for students from Illinois and $17,261 for others. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of Eastern Illinois University?

50% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
