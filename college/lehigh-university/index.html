Lehigh University: cost, admission and results

Lehigh University is a private nonprofit college in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania. It has about 5,898 undergraduate students. It admits 26% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $25,272 a year.

Getting in

Lehigh University admits 26% of those who apply, which makes it selective. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%. The average SAT score of admitted students is 1440 out of 1600 (midpoints: 688 in reading and writing, 720 in maths). The midpoint ACT score is 32 out of 36.

The students

21% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 36% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 48% women and 52% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 21 of the 154 colleges in Pennsylvania on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,488.

Common questions

What is the acceptance rate of Lehigh University?

Lehigh University admits 26% of applicants, according to the College Scorecard.

How much is tuition at Lehigh University?

Published tuition and fees are $67,920 a year. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of Lehigh University?

85% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
