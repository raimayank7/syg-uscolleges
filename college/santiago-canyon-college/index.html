Santiago Canyon College: cost, admission and results

Santiago Canyon College is a public college in Orange, California. It has about 10,579 undergraduate students. The average net price for students with federal aid is $2,040 a year.

Getting in

Santiago Canyon College has an open admission policy: it does not turn applicants away on grades or test scores, though some courses can have their own requirements.

The net price is what students who got federal aid paid on average for a year after grants and scholarships were taken off (for a public college, students from inside the state). It is 91% below the median net price of colleges in California on this site ($22,336). International students are normally charged the out-of-state tuition and do not receive US federal aid.

The students

12% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 0% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 35% women and 65% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 55 of the 267 colleges in California on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 2,759.

Common questions

How much is tuition at Santiago Canyon College?

Published tuition and fees are $1,160 a year for students from California and $11,384 for others. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of Santiago Canyon College?

48% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
