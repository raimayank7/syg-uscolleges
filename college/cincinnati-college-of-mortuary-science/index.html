The students

47% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 72% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 82% women and 18% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 110 of the 117 colleges in Ohio on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,006.

What is not reported

The College Scorecard has no figure for an admission rate, published tuition, a net price, the share of students who return for a second year, a graduation rate or loan debt at Cincinnati College of Mortuary Science. That is common for colleges that are small or new, that teach mostly graduate students, or that take few first-time, full-time students, because several measures count only that group. It says nothing by itself about quality. Ask the college directly for the figures that matter to you.
