The colleges of Wyoming in brief

Of the 8 colleges listed for Wyoming, 8 are public. By level, 1 mainly awards bachelor's degrees and 7 mainly award two-year associate degrees. Together they teach about 18,302 undergraduates, 100% of them at public colleges. The three largest, University of Wyoming, Laramie County Community College and Casper College, hold 71% of the total, and the middle-sized college has 1,464 undergraduates.

What college costs in Wyoming

At the public colleges, the median published tuition and fees are $4,770 a year for students from Wyoming and $11,070 for students from elsewhere. Published prices are not what most families pay. Counting colleges with at least 300 undergraduates, the lowest average net price after grants is at Eastern Wyoming College ($3,986) and Northwest College ($7,660) and the highest at University of Wyoming ($13,647) and Central Wyoming College ($12,055). The median net price across the state, $9,611, is 45% below the national median of $17,357.

Getting in

1 of the 8 colleges reports an admission rate. 7 colleges have an open admission policy and report no rate: they do not turn applicants away on grades or test scores.

Finishing and earning

Among colleges with at least 300 undergraduates, the highest share of students finishing their course is at University of Wyoming (61%), Casper College (44%) and Eastern Wyoming College (42%). The median graduation rate of the state's colleges is 41%, against 49% for the country. Ten years after starting, former students with federal aid earn the most at University of Wyoming ($62,764), Laramie County Community College ($47,827) and Western Wyoming Community College ($46,249). The state median for that measure is $45,300, 15% below the national $53,132. Students who finish leave with a median federal loan debt of $9,868 (national median $19,994). Earnings follow the subjects a college teaches as much as its quality, so compare like with like.

Questions about colleges in Wyoming

How many colleges are there in Wyoming?

This site lists 8 open, degree-granting colleges and universities in Wyoming, out of 3,161 on the whole site. Schools that mainly give short certificates are not counted.

What is the largest college in Wyoming?

University of Wyoming in Laramie, with about 7,944 undergraduate students.

Which college in Wyoming has the lowest net price?

Among colleges with at least 300 undergraduates, Eastern Wyoming College: students with federal aid paid an average of $3,986 a year after grants. Students from other states or countries usually pay more.
