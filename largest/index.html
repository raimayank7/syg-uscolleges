Reading this list

  • The count covers undergraduates only. Graduate students are not included.
  • A branch campus is counted separately from its main campus where the Department of Education reports it separately.

What the list is made of

The 100 colleges are in 35 states and territories; the most are in California (18), Texas (17), Florida (8) and Arizona (6). By type, 89 public, 7 private nonprofit and 4 private for-profit. Sizes run from 163,164 undergraduates down to 21,996. Together the 100 colleges teach about 3,631,172 undergraduates.

What else the figures show

Large colleges are mostly easy to enter: the median admission rate among those that report one is 71%. 22 report none, 22 of them because they have an open admission policy. The median net price is $14,851 a year (all colleges: $17,357). Graduation rates differ widely among them, from 94% at University of California-Berkeley to 17% at Houston City College.
