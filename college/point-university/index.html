Point University: cost, admission and results

Point University is a private nonprofit college in West Point, Georgia. It has about 1,996 undergraduate students. It admits 44% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $24,955 a year.

Getting in

Point University admits 44% of those who apply, which makes it moderately selective. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%. The midpoint ACT score is 19 out of 36.

The students

50% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 20% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 49% women and 51% men.

Common questions

What is the acceptance rate of Point University?

Point University admits 44% of applicants, according to the College Scorecard.

How much is tuition at Point University?

Published tuition and fees are $24,300 a year. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of Point University?

60% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
