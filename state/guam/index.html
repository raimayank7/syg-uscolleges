The colleges of Guam in brief

Of the 3 colleges listed for Guam, 2 are public and 1 is private nonprofit. By level, 1 mainly awards bachelor's degrees and 2 mainly award two-year associate degrees. All of them are in Mangilao. With so few colleges, each can be named: University of Guam in Mangilao is public, with about 2,515 undergraduates and published tuition and fees of $6,110 a year. Guam Community College in Mangilao is public, with about 1,139 undergraduates and published tuition and fees of $3,414 a year. Pacific Islands University in Mangilao is private nonprofit, with about 61 undergraduates and published tuition and fees of $7,203 a year.

Getting in

1 of the 3 colleges reports an admission rate. 2 colleges have an open admission policy and report no rate: they do not turn applicants away on grades or test scores.
