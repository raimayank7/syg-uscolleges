Family of Faith Christian University: cost, admission and results

Family of Faith Christian University is a private nonprofit college in Shawnee, Oklahoma. It has about 82 undergraduate students. It admits 76% of applicants.

Getting in

Family of Faith Christian University admits 76% of those who apply, which makes it open to most applicants. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%.

What it costs

Tuition and fees for a year$9,660
Room and meals on campus$10,624
Books and supplies$1,320

International students normally pay the published tuition and do not receive US federal aid.

For an estimate that fits your own family, use the college's net price calculator.

The students

39% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 19% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 62% women and 38% men.
