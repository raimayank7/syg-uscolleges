University of Florida-Online: cost, admission and results

University of Florida-Online is a public college in Gainesville, Florida. It has about 4,627 undergraduate students. It admits 61% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $4,336 a year.

Getting in

University of Florida-Online admits 61% of those who apply, which makes it moderately selective. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%. The average SAT score of admitted students is 1338 out of 1600 (midpoints: 655 in reading and writing, 645 in maths). The midpoint ACT score is 29 out of 36.

The students

24% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 11% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 56% women and 44% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 36 of the 118 colleges in Florida on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,413.

Common questions

What is the acceptance rate of University of Florida-Online?

University of Florida-Online admits 61% of applicants, according to the College Scorecard.

How much is tuition at University of Florida-Online?

Published tuition and fees are $3,876 a year for students from Florida and $16,579 for others. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of University of Florida-Online?

83% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
