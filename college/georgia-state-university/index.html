Georgia State University: cost, admission and results

Georgia State University is a public college in Atlanta, Georgia. It has about 26,623 undergraduate students. It admits 55% of applicants. The average net price for students with federal aid is $12,365 a year.

Getting in

Georgia State University admits 55% of those who apply, which makes it moderately selective. The median admission rate of colleges on this site that report one is 77%. The average SAT score of admitted students is 1076 out of 1600 (midpoints: 590 in reading and writing, 580 in maths). The midpoint ACT score is 23 out of 36.

The students

57% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 39% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 59% women and 41% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 3 of the 69 colleges in Georgia on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,996.

Common questions

What is the acceptance rate of Georgia State University?

Georgia State University admits 55% of applicants, according to the College Scorecard.

How much is tuition at Georgia State University?

Published tuition and fees are $8,664 a year for students from Georgia and $25,320 for others. Most students with aid pay less: see the net price.

What is the graduation rate of Georgia State University?

51% of first-time, full-time students finish within one and a half times the normal length of the course.
