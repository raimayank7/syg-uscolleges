Antigua College International: cost, admission and results

Antigua College International is a private for-profit college in Miami Lakes, Florida. It has about 251 undergraduate students. The average net price for students with federal aid is $41,790 a year.

Getting in

Antigua College International has an open admission policy: it does not turn applicants away on grades or test scores, though some courses can have their own requirements.

What it costs

The net price is what students who got federal aid paid on average for a year after grants and scholarships were taken off. It is 85% above the median net price of colleges in Florida on this site ($22,575). International students normally pay the published tuition and do not receive US federal aid.

The students

66% of undergraduates receive a Pell Grant, the federal grant for students from lower-income families. 100% take a federal student loan. The undergraduate body is 80% women and 20% men. By undergraduate numbers it is number 100 of the 118 colleges in Florida on this site that report a size; the middle-sized one has 1,413.

What is not reported

The College Scorecard has no figure for an admission rate, a graduation rate or earnings of former students at Antigua College International. That is common for colleges that are small or new, that teach mostly graduate students, or that take few first-time, full-time students, because several measures count only that group. It says nothing by itself about quality. Ask the college directly for the figures that matter to you.
